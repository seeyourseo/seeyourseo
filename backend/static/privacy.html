Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice explains how SeeYourSEO handles information when you use the website audit service. SeeYourSEO is operated independently by an individual, not by a company. For general questions, email hello@seeyourseo.com. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@seeyourseo.com.

Scans are currently free with no fixed free-tier page cap and do not require an account or payment. Actual coverage depends on site structure and available service capacity. Checkout for new purchases is paused. We retain payment records needed to support earlier purchases. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Information used to provide a scan

When you request a scan, we process the website address you submit, the pages and links discovered on that website, page response details, and the resulting audit report. A scan may include information that is present on the public pages you ask us to inspect. Please scan only websites you own or are authorized to review.

Why we process it

We use this information to perform the scan you requested and provide its report. This processing is necessary to provide the requested service. We also have a legitimate interest in maintaining the security and reliability of the service. We do not use scan content to build advertising profiles or sell it.

Website access logs

When a public website page is requested, we may record the visitor IP address provided by Cloudflare, the page path without query parameters, the time of the request, and the HTTP response status. We rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the service from abuse, maintaining reliability, and understanding basic page usage. Logs are available only to the service operator through the password-protected dashboard and are automatically deleted within 7 days. We do not use these records for cross-site tracking or advertising. Requests served from Cloudflare's cache without reaching our server may not appear in these logs.

Earlier purchases and accounts

Checkout for new purchases is currently paused. For scans purchased earlier, we retain the email address, selected plan, amount, currency, payment status, PayPal order and capture references, and account sign-in/session data needed to provide existing scan credits, prevent duplicate credits, answer payment questions, and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations. PayPal processed those transactions; SeeYourSEO does not receive or store your PayPal password or card number. Contact privacy@seeyourseo.com to ask about an earlier purchase or your personal data.

Earlier unfinished checkout records are deleted within 30 days after checkout expires. Payment records needed to verify a previous transaction are kept separately for the period required by applicable accounting and legal rules. Expired sign-in sessions are removed.

Email messages

SeeYourSEO does not send transactional or marketing email. Email-only account access for earlier purchases does not verify inbox ownership.

Retention and report links

The report and its pages are held in temporary server memory and the report link expires 20 minutes after the scan finishes. The public IANA sample report is a static example generated from public pages and is separate from your submitted scan report. Anyone with your report link may view that report while it remains available, so share it only with people you trust. The scan’s background crawl and analysis task records are automatically removed after their configured task-retention period, which is one hour by default. A server restart can remove temporary reports sooner. To monitor service use, we separately retain the submitted website hostname, scan time, status, and page count for up to 30 days; these scan records do not contain visitor IP addresses or full URLs. Website access logs described above contain IP addresses and are kept for up to 7 days. Both sets of records are visible only to the service operator in the password-protected dashboard. Downloaded Markdown files are saved by you and are outside our control. Your browser may keep the report URL in its local browsing history until you clear it.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website does not set optional analytics, advertising, or preference cookies. Sign-in for earlier scan credits uses a strictly necessary, secure, HttpOnly session cookie. The current checkout page does not load PayPal software. PayPal may continue to process information connected with earlier transactions under its own privacy notice. The browser address bar can contain a temporary report link. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tracking; limited server-side access logging is described above.

Service providers and website destinations

We use the configured hosting provider and MariaDB database. PayPal may process records connected with earlier purchases under its own terms. During a scan, requests are made to the website and link destinations you ask us to check; those sites receive ordinary network request information and apply their own privacy terms.

International transfers

Scan data is processed on the SeeYourSEO server. PayPal may process information about earlier transactions in other countries under its terms and applicable transfer safeguards.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since reports expire automatically, the easiest way to remove an active report early or ask a question is to email privacy@seeyourseo.com. We may need enough information to identify the report, such as its report URL. We will not ask you to send passwords or unrelated sensitive data.

Security and changes

Reports are held temporarily in memory and are not intended as permanent storage. Payment and account records are stored in the service database; expired verification and sign-in tokens are not valid. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change; the date above will be revised when we do.

Contact

SeeYourSEO is independently operated by an individual. Privacy contact: privacy@seeyourseo.com.
