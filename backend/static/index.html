Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about SeeYourSEO.

Is SeeYourSEO open source?

Yes. The free edition is licensed under MIT, and its source is available on GitHub.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. Clone the MIT-licensed repository and follow its build and service setup guide. Linux deployments require Landlock filesystem ABI v1.

How is it different from Screaming Frog?

SeeYourSEO is a browser-based audit with no fixed per-scan page cap in the free edition, subject to service capacity. Screaming Frog’s free SEO Spider is a desktop crawler with a 500-URL crawl limit. We have not published a controlled head-to-head test.

Is the free scan really free?

Yes. No account is required, and there is no fixed per-scan page cap. Coverage depends on site structure and service capacity; reports are available for 20 minutes.
