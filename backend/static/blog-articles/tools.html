SEO resources / Tool directory

SEO tools, with a job in mind.

SEO tools can speed up keyword research, competitor analysis, page speed checks, and measurement. Start with a question, then validate decisions with audience evidence. Estimates are useful signals, not ground truth.

ToolUse it forKeep in mind
Google TrendsCompare interest over time.Relative interest, not exact search volume.
Google Keyword PlannerKeyword ideas and volume ranges.Ranges can be broad.
AhrefsKeyword, backlink, and competitor research.Third-party estimates are modeled.
SemrushKeyword and advertising research.Comparative estimates are not exact traffic.
SimilarwebEstimate competitor traffic sources.Coverage varies by site.
Google Search ConsoleReview your queries, clicks, and indexing.Requires verified site ownership.
Chrome UX ReportReview real-user page experience data.Some pages have no field data.
Cloudflare RadarExplore public internet and domain trends.Broad signals, not ranking data.
DataForSEOUse keyword and search results APIs.Paid and usage-based.
SerpApiRetrieve structured search result data.Provider limits apply.
Common CrawlExplore a public web archive.Large datasets need technical setup.

Check current provider pricing, terms, and coverage before relying on an SEO research workflow. For your own website, a focused technical SEO scan can complement keyword and competitor research.
